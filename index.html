Meet Frady

Frady is a Persian cat with a lot of fur and very little patience.

He is a fictional, animated cat who lives in Brooklyn. He shops for ingredients at the bodega, then cooks in his apartment. He never says a word.

  • MoodGrumpy. Fluffy. Usually hungry.
  • CooksSteak and eggs, sushi, ramen, mochi
  • PlaysRecords, keys, and naps
